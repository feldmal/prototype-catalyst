ABOUT PROTOTYPE CATALYST

Practical solutions
for real business work.

We work with businesses to improve the way information moves through their operations—from the spreadsheets and recurring tasks behind the scenes to the reporting that supports better decisions.

Our approach

Start with the problem.
Build what solves it.

Every business has processes that have grown over time: spreadsheets passed between teams, recurring reports assembled by hand, and data stored in places that don't easily work together.

Prototype Catalyst is a custom services consultancy focused on data automation and analytics. We start by understanding the work, not by trying to fit your business into a prebuilt product. Then we design and build the tools, workflows, and reporting that address the actual problem.

That might mean replacing a complex Excel macro, combining information from several sources, building an employee hours and job-costing workflow, or creating a detailed dashboard for managers. The right scope depends on your process and goals.
