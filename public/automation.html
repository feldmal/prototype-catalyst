Beyond the spreadsheet

Keep the familiar inputs.
Improve what happens next.

Many teams rely on Excel workbooks and CSV exports to move information between systems. As files grow and steps multiply, desktop macros can become slow, difficult to maintain, and dependent on a particular computer.

We first map the steps, rules, and exceptions in your current process. Then we build and test a workflow that handles the processing consistently. Depending on the project, results can be delivered as files, prepared for another system, or connected to a reporting dashboard.
