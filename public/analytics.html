Reporting that works for you

Useful reporting starts
with reliable data.

A useful dashboard starts with understanding the business: which measures matter, how they are calculated, where the data comes from, and who needs to use the results. We work through those questions before deciding how the reporting should be structured.

Projects can begin with Excel and CSV files, with data preparation and modeling included as needed. We can also discuss managed hosting and recurring refreshes so the reporting remains available as part of your operation.

BUILT FOR YOUR BUSINESS

Different teams need different views.

The example above is only one possible direction. Dashboards can be tailored to the metrics, workflow, and level of detail each business needs.

Sales & customers

Revenue, pipeline, order volume, product mix, customer activity, and performance against goals.

Operations & workforce

Employee hours, utilization, workload, scheduling, throughput, backlog, and service levels.

Projects & finance

Job costs, budgets, profitability, inventory movement, purchasing, and resource allocation.

These are examples of possible custom reporting—not a fixed catalog of products or templates. Dashboard structure, measures, access, and refresh schedules are scoped to each engagement.
