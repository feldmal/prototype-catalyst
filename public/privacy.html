WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

What information is collected when you contact Prototype Catalyst through this website, and how it is used.

Current status: Prototype Catalyst is a working business name for a business still in development. This notice describes the current marketing website and inquiry form, not a future customer portal or data-processing service.

Information you submit

If you use the project inquiry form, you may provide your name, business name, email address, service interest, budget range, timeline, and project description. The form also includes a hidden anti-spam field. The form does not accept file uploads.

How the information is used

Information submitted through the form is used to review and respond to your inquiry, discuss potential project requirements, and maintain related business correspondence. Do not submit passwords, sensitive personal information, customer records, payment-card information, or confidential business data through this form.

Service providers

The website is hosted using Cloudflare Workers and website assets. Form submissions are transmitted to the email delivery provider Resend so the inquiry can be delivered to the configured project inbox. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and requests

Inquiry information may remain in the receiving email inbox and related correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, evaluate a potential engagement, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion of an inquiry you submitted, contact feldmal@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping.

Cookies and analytics

The current website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party web analytics. Hosting providers may process technical information such as request metadata and security logs as part of delivering and protecting the site.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the website and inquiry process. Email is not an appropriate channel for highly sensitive information, and no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

External links and future services

This website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Any future customer workspace, file uploads, managed data hosting, or dashboard services will require separate service terms and appropriate privacy and data-processing documentation before customer data is accepted.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated as the business and website develop. For privacy questions, contact feldmal@gmail.com.

Last updated: November 20, 2026. This notice is an operational website notice, not legal advice or a substitute for a reviewed privacy policy before commercial launch.
